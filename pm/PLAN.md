# Plan — wrap-up and refinement

Approved 2026-11-07 (Idan). Lead: Claude. Owners of record: Gal Machlin (macwave), Idan Yehuda.

## Goal

Present the **existing** project (TDA + margin admission, probabilistic fusion, gated fusion,
LOO temperature) to the professor as a rigorous, evidence-backed piece of work:

- Project goal, stated precisely: **zero-shot CLIP classification** — a known set of class
  names, an unlabeled test stream, no labeled images, no backprop. Improve the
  trustworthiness (calibration) of test-time adaptation without giving up its accuracy.
- Every claim on a slide is backed by a number from a CSV or a figure generated by code.
- Fair comparisons: results under two hyperparameter protocols (below).
- Positioned against the literature: the method landscape, ≤2 more zero-shot methods run
  by us, 1 few-shot method, and reported numbers for calibration-specific methods.

Out of scope until the wrap-up is finished: new methods (reservoir cache, soft-label cache,
self-vote removal). They are planned in `docs/plans/` and `research/`.

## Hyperparameter protocols

- **P1 "published"**: TDA's per-dataset α/β from the official repo (comparable to the
  benchmark and the TDA paper). Our knobs at their a-priori values.
- **P2 "clean"**: α = 2, β = 5 on every dataset for every method (TDA's own ImageNet-val
  setting, supp. Table 6). Our knobs at their a-priori values. No method has seen any of the
  five test sets.
- Decision rule (Idan, 2026-11-07): if P2-TDA beats zero-shot CLIP's accuracy on the
  datasets, P2 is the headline protocol and P1 is shown for comparability; otherwise P1 is
  the headline and P2 is shown as the honest zero-shot check. Both are always reported.

## Compute strategy (no GPU)

CLIP is frozen and every method we run uses a single centre-crop view, so image features
are a pure function of the image. Plan:

1. **One GPU job** (macwave, ~10–15 min): extract ViT-B/16 image features for the test and
   val splits of the five datasets and a 16-shot train subset, plus text features, with the
   exact preprocessing and fp16 autocast of the original runs, and the stream permutations
   for seeds 0/1/2. (`T01`)
2. Everything else runs on **CPU** by replaying cached features through the unchanged
   method code (`T02`). The replay must reproduce `results/summary.csv` before any new
   number is trusted.

## Deck outline (target ~33 slides; TDA ≥ 25%)

1–2 title, agenda · 3–7 background (problem setting; CLIP, both encoders frozen, prompt
"a photo of a {class}."; why it fails: domain shift and caption granularity; method
landscape; why TDA) · 8–15 TDA in depth (idea; write rule; read rule and α/β; negative
cache; final logits = product of experts; worked example; datasets with CLIP vs TDA
accuracy/ECE; where TDA's gain comes from) · 16–19 calibration (ECE and reliability; why it
matters; CLIP vs TDA confidence/ECE/reliability; anatomy of TDA's overconfidence) · 20 our
proposal · 21–28 each change: motive → why it should work → evidence → result · 29–31
results, protocols P1/P2, ablation, baselines · 32–33 limitations and next steps,
conclusion · backup.

## Acceptance criteria for the deck

1. Every number on a slide is produced by code from a tracked CSV (no hand-typed numbers).
2. Every mechanism claim has a figure or table on the same or the next slide.
3. Each proposed change states its motive and a concrete example of why it should work.
4. The method landscape and the protocol choice answer "why only TDA?" and "is it fair?".
5. Limitations slide: seeds, backbone, selection history, estimator quality.
6. All three reviewers report no blocking issue; Idan signs off.
