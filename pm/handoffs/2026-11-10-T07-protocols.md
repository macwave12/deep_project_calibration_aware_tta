## Result
- **Rule → P2.** On the dataset mean, P2-TDA beats CLIP by +3.66 accuracy, on 3/3 seeds.
  It also wins on every dataset's mean; Aircraft is the weakest (+0.12, 2/3 seeds).
- The clean protocol does not hurt TDA: mean acc 56.78 (P2) vs 56.60 (P1). Per-dataset α/β
  mostly redistribute ECE between datasets.
- Our method's story is the same under P2. Gate trades about 2 points of accuracy for about
  6.5 points of ECE, and temperature is the only variant that wins both metrics on 3/3
  seeds (+0.03 acc, −3.24 ECE). Margin and ungated fusion lose on both.

  Pets ECE regression (about +12.8 vs TDA) holds under both protocols.

  It is a property of the gate, not of α/β.
