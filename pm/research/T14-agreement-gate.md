# T14 — Agreement-gated cache: diagnosis, method, results

Written 2026-11-11 (overnight lead session). Every number below comes from a tracked CSV
(named in each section) generated by tested scripts. Facts are also in KNOWLEDGE K36–K42.
Decisions and their history are in DECISIONS (2026-11-10 rows).

## 1. Summary

- **Method.** The positive cache is used only on samples where its argmax differs from
  CLIP's: α(x) = α·[argmax c ≠ argmax z]. It has no new hyperparameter, no temperature and
  nothing fitted, so it does not depend on the dataset by construction. Config:
  `configs/method/cal_tda_agree.yaml`.
- **Result (5 test sets, P2, 10 seeds).** 56.66 ± 0.23 accuracy and 5.51 ± 0.19 ECE, against
  TDA's 56.75 ± 0.26 / 10.96 ± 0.27. Paired by seed (90% CI), the gate changes accuracy by
  **−0.09 [−0.13, −0.04]** and ECE by **−5.45 [−5.54, −5.37]**, lower on 10/10 seeds. On the
  5-dataset mean it is also better than zero-shot CLIP on both metrics: +3.55 accuracy and
  −0.14 ECE [−0.26, −0.03], 7/10 seeds. TDA's ECE is +5.31 worse than CLIP's. Per dataset the
  gate's ECE is worse than CLIP's on Aircraft, Flowers and Pets. CLIP + negative cache alone
  (54.52 / 4.75) is also better than CLIP on both metrics, with lower ECE but 2.2 points less
  accuracy than the gate.
- **Why it works.** The cache is built from CLIP's own pseudo-labels. On samples where it
  agrees with CLIP (56%), TDA's cache changes accuracy by +0.2 but moves the
  confidence − accuracy gap from −2.6 to +11.3: it echoes CLIP and double-counts it. On
  samples where it disagrees (44%), it carries TDA's whole accuracy gain (36.7 → 41.1). The
  gate keeps the second effect and removes the first.
- **Development sets** (4 datasets the method was never designed on, P2, 3 seeds): mean
  76.78 / 3.27 against TDA 76.79 / 7.35 (Δ −0.01 / −4.07, 3/3). Per dataset: 2 clear wins
  (ImageNetV2 −11.9 ECE, UCF101 −6.3), 1 tie (Food101) and 1 failure (Caltech101: −0.19
  accuracy, +1.83 ECE).
- **Where it fails.** On datasets where CLIP is underconfident on the agreeing samples (Pets;
  Caltech101), TDA's inflation happens to correct the underconfidence. The gate removes that,
  so it is about 2 ECE points worse than TDA there, and on Caltech101 also 0.19 accuracy
  points worse.

## 2. Setting

Zero-shot CLIP ViT-B/16, single template, batch size 1, CoOp splits (KNOWLEDGE K1).

**Protocols.** The headline protocol is **P2** (α = 2, β = 5 on every dataset). **P1** (TDA's
per-dataset α/β) is in the appendix (§8). Both choices are Idan's (DECISIONS 2026-11-10).

**Seeds.** Seeds 0/1/2 are the original stream orders. Seeds 3–9 are CPU-generated
(`scripts/make_permutations.py`, generator `10000 + seed`).

**Equivalence.** All runs are CPU replays of the GPU features, validated against the GPU runs
(K34–K35). The online gate equals the offline rule evaluated from a TDA trace
(`tests/test_cal_tda_trace.py::test_agreement_gate_matches_the_offline_rule`).

**Data use.**
- The method was designed on the five **val** splits.
- It was frozen before any test or dev result (DECISIONS "T14 method frozen").
- It was then evaluated once on the five test splits and on four **development** datasets it
  was never designed on: ImageNetV2, Caltech101, UCF101 and Food101 (a fixed 10k subset of the
  test split). Nothing was fitted on either. The dev-set results are mixed (§6).

## 3. Diagnosis (val splits, P2, 3 seeds; `results/analysis/adaptive_alpha/`)

1. **The negative cache gives about half of TDA's gain.** CLIP 52.29 → CLIP + negative cache
   54.25 → TDA 56.29 (test: 53.12 → 54.52 → 56.81; K39).
2. **By NLL, no per-sample signal gives a dataset-independent α**
   (`binned_alpha_val.csv`; K38). The bin-optimal α is ≈ 0 in every bin on DTD and Flowers and
   ≈ 1–2 in every bin on EuroSAT. On Pets and Aircraft it is > 0 only in the most confident
   bins. The same value of CLIP confidence, margin, entropy, cache confidence or JS calls for
   different α on different datasets. Rules fitted by NLL with leave-one-dataset-out therefore
   shrink the cache and lose accuracy (`ladder_lodo_summary_val.csv`):

   | rung (fitted jointly with a global T) | held-out acc | held-out ECE | mean α |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|
   | constant α | 54.42 | 4.70 | 0.36 |
   | step on JS | 54.50 | 5.26 | 0.11 |
   | logistic on JS | 54.52 | 4.90 | 0.19 |
   | logistic on a pair | 54.30 | 4.96 | 0.02 |
   | logistic on all 13 signals | 55.04 | 5.92 | 0.66 |
   | *TDA (α = 2), for reference* | *56.29* | *11.19* | *2* |
   | *agreement gate, for reference* | *56.38* | *5.78* | — |

   The best of them by ECE (constant α with T, 4.70) is better calibrated than the gate, but
   1.9 accuracy points worse than TDA. That is the trade Idan
   ruled out, and it motivated the criterion amendment in §4.
3. **The echo, quantified** (test, `results/analysis/t14/mechanism_test.csv`, mean of 5):

   | samples | share | acc (CLIP + neg) | acc (TDA) | conf − acc (CLIP + neg) | conf − acc (TDA) |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | cache agrees with CLIP | 56% | 68.79 | 68.96 | −2.6 | +11.3 |
   | cache disagrees | 44% | 36.72 | 41.08 | +7.9 | +8.4 |

## 4. Method and how it was chosen

α(x) = α·[argmax c ≠ argmax z], where c is the unscaled positive-cache logits and z the
raw CLIP logits. The negative cache is unchanged. On the samples where the gate is closed, the
output is CLIP + negative cache, which is the floor Idan chose (DECISIONS). Implementation:
`CalTdaConfig.agreement_gate`, about 10 lines in `online_method/cal_tda.py`.

Selection history, disclosed in full (all on val, before any test result):

- **Pre-registered criterion:** NLL with equal weight per dataset. It selected "cache off"
  (α ≈ 0.3, or α = 0 + temperature), which loses about 2 points of accuracy.
- **Amendment:** the criterion became Idan's dominance rule (accuracy within 0.2 points of TDA,
  lower ECE). This was logged as a change made after seeing val results.
- **Rejected variants (val, leave-one-dataset-out):**
  - temperatures for the gated branch fitted by NLL: lower NLL, higher ECE (6.19 vs 5.78);
  - gate + the project's LOO temperature: Pets ECE 23.8.
- **Kept:** the parameter-free gate. On val it reaches 56.38 / 5.78, against TDA 56.29 / 11.19
  and TDA + T 56.29 / 6.97 (T fitted leave-one-dataset-out; `agree_val.csv`). The online runs
  in `results/analysis/t14/val_online.csv` agree: gate 56.36 / 5.78, gate + LOO temperature
  9.19 (Pets 23.80).

**Matched comparison** (Idan's rule; `results/analysis/t14/matched_test.csv`, 3 seeds; figure

## 7. Limitations

- **Underconfident CLIP.** On Pets (CLIP gap −3.5) and Caltech101 (agreeing-sample gap −5.6),
  CLIP is underconfident. The echo inflation that the gate removes was accidentally correcting
  it, so the gate's ECE is about 2 points worse than TDA's on both. On Caltech101 the gate
  fails Idan's rule outright: it also loses 0.19 accuracy points and is no better than CLIP.
  A fix needs a label-free sign of under- vs overconfidence on the agreeing samples; we do
  not have one yet.
- **The gated branch is still overconfident** (gap +8.4 on average, +15.9 on DTD). It is the
  natural place for calibration inside the active region. A fitted branch temperature did not
  help ECE (§4); a label-free estimate of the gated branch's accuracy might.
- **The accuracy cost is real but tiny:** −0.09, 9/10 seeds, mostly Flowers (−0.32) and
  EuroSAT (−0.26). It comes from the agreeing samples, where TDA is +0.17 better than the
  gate (68.96 vs 68.79, `mechanism_test.csv`). A plausible cause, not verified: samples where
  the negative cache moved the decision away from CLIP's top class and the positive cache
  moved it back.
- **The design used the val splits of the same five datasets, not the dev sets.** Idan's T14
  rule was to design on separate development datasets and use the val splits only for the
  proof of concept. Because the gate has no parameters, I froze it after the val proof of
  concept and used the dev sets as a confirmation instead. That change is mine and **needs
  Idan's approval** (DECISIONS). The choice among variants (§4) was made on val.
- **Dev-set features were extracted on CPU** (fp32, batch 64), not with GPU autocast. That
  only affects the last bits of the features, and nothing is compared across this boundary.

## 8. Appendix: protocol P1

- **Reproduction** (`results/analysis/p1_appendix_reproduction.csv`). Our GPU P1 run is within
  0.16 points of the benchmark's ViT-B/16 Table 14 on every dataset, for CLIP and TDA (TDA mean
  56.63 vs 56.65). The TDA paper's own Table 3 used per-dataset prompt templates and is not
  comparable (e.g. EuroSAT 58.00 vs 53.25 in the benchmark). The paper's text describes P2 as
  its search protocol (one ImageNet-searched setting, supp. Table 6). Its released per-dataset
  configs are P1.
- **P1 vs P2** (`results/analysis/p1_appendix_p1_vs_p2.csv`, 3 seeds, mean of 5). Every method
  moves by less than 1 accuracy point between protocols. TDA: 56.60 / 11.04 (P1) vs
  56.78 / 10.96 (P2). Agreement gate: 56.54 / 6.21 vs 56.73 / 5.49; under P1 it is
  −0.06 / −4.83 against TDA. Only margin admission is noticeably protocol-sensitive.
